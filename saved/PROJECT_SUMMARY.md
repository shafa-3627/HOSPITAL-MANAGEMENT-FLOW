### 🚀 Key Features to Showcase to Judges

1. **Flow Health Score (0–100)**: Real-time dynamic gauge calculated from bed occupancy, ED queue length, staff workload, and active crisis alerts.
2. **Interactive 13-Step Judge Demo Tour**: Click **"Start Judge Demo"** in the Command Center or Header to trigger the deterministic guided walkthrough across all key features.
3. **Interactive Surge Stress Scenarios**: Click *ICU Surge*, *Mass Casualty*, *Staff Shortage*, or *Weekend Surge* on the Command Center to watch models predict capacity saturation in real-time.
4. **Digital Twin What-If Simulator**: Test temporary bed expansions, staffing adjustments, and expedited discharges with Before vs. After metrics.
5. **Grounded AI Copilot**: Ask clinical operational questions; answers are generated directly from live SQLite database telemetry with evidence cards.
6. **Prescriptive Recommendations**: View AI-generated optimizations with a human-in-the-loop approval workflow and immutable audit logging.
